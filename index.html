FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and booking process.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online using the appointment form above, call our clinic directly at +1 (800) CARDIO, or email us at care@cardiocare.com. We confirm all appointments within 2 business hours.

What heart conditions do you treat?

We treat a comprehensive range of cardiovascular conditions including coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, hypertension, valvular heart disease, peripheral artery disease, and more.

Do you offer emergency consultations?

Yes, our emergency cardiac care line is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. If you're experiencing chest pain, shortness of breath, or other acute cardiac symptoms, please call our emergency team immediately.

What insurance plans are accepted?

We accept most major insurance plans including Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealth, and many more. Please contact our billing team to verify your specific plan coverage.

How long does a first consultation take?

An initial consultation typically takes 45–60 minutes. This includes a detailed medical history review, physical examination, and if needed, initial diagnostic tests such as an ECG.

Do you offer telemedicine consultations?

Yes, we offer secure video consultations for follow-up visits, medication reviews, and initial assessments where no physical examination is required. Telemedicine appointments are available Monday through Saturday, 9AM to 5PM.
